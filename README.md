# LESO — AI-Assisted Engineering Documentation Platform

> Public project case study. Source code, client data and confidential implementation details are not published.

## Overview

LESO is a digital platform designed to reduce the amount of repetitive documentation work performed by engineering teams.

The product centralizes project information and assists with the preparation of technical, cost-estimation and regulatory deliverables, while keeping the engineer responsible for validation and final sign-off.

## Business problem

Engineering teams can spend a significant amount of time reproducing the same project information across several documents, spreadsheets, regulatory files and reports.

The objective of LESO is to reduce:

- repetitive data entry;
- document reformatting;
- duplicated project information;
- manual preparation of recurring deliverables;
- time spent on administrative and regulatory documentation.

## Main capabilities

### Technical specifications — SIA 451
Generation support for structured technical descriptions by lot, with SIA 451 references and export-ready documents.

### DPGF / Cost estimation
Structured cost-estimation output with quantities and unit prices, designed to support tender preparation.

### Vaud building-permit documentation
Assistance with the preparation of APC documentation, supporting material, SIA 416 data and CAMAC-related checklists.

### SIA 380/1 workflow
Preparation of project data and formatted reporting around the official calculation workflow.

> Official calculations remain in the dedicated professional tool. LESO does not replace the engineer's technical responsibility.

### Rapid project simulation
Early-stage estimation to support project comparison and design discussions.

### AEAI checklist
Structured compliance checklist and reporting according to project typology.

### Meeting minutes
Transformation of meeting notes into structured summaries containing decisions, actions and owners.

### Regulatory monitoring
Project-oriented monitoring of relevant Vaud / Swiss requirements such as MoPEC, LEn-VD, Minergie and SIA references.

## Product approach

The platform was designed around a simple principle:

**enter project information once, reuse it across multiple deliverables.**

Instead of rebuilding each document independently, project data can be reused by specialized modules.

## Human-in-the-loop

LESO is an assistance platform, not an autonomous engineering authority.

- AI prepares and structures information.
- The engineer reviews and validates critical content.
- Official calculations remain in the relevant professional tools.
- Final responsibility and sign-off remain with the qualified professional.

## Illustrative business case

For one modeled multi-lot residential project, the initial workflow represented approximately **44 working days** across several deliverables.

The target workflow with LESO was estimated at approximately **3.5 working days**, representing roughly **40 days of potential capacity recovered**.

These figures were used as a project business-case estimate and should not be interpreted as audited production results.
